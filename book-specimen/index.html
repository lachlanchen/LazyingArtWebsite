A deliberately small first step

Your words stay your words.

You supply final edited copy and hold the necessary rights. If the chapter is bilingual, you also supply the final aligned translation. The sprint handles structure, layout, EPUB packaging, and production checks; it does not include translation, substantive editing, illustration, cover design, ISBNs, marketplace upload, printing, or a complete book.

You provide

One chapter up to 5,000 words, final text in DOCX, Markdown, or clean text, no more than twelve supplied images, captions and credits, the primary language, and an optional already-aligned companion language.

We agree first

The print colour mode, trim and margin assumptions, language order, image handling, accessibility notes, source-transfer method, retention, deadline, and exact output list.

You keep control

You approve every textual change. Your source is not published, reused as a sample, uploaded to a marketplace, or sent to a printer through this sprint.

Working terms

Know the boundary before files move.

The accepted written scope controls the work for a particular chapter.

01

Timing

Delivery is ten business days after scope acceptance, confirmed payment, and receipt of the complete agreed source. Only one Book Specimen Sprint is active at a time; the clock pauses while a required input is missing.

02

Correction pass

Send one consolidated list of up to ten formatting, source-mapping, navigation, or packaging corrections within seven calendar days after delivery. New text, translation, another print profile, or another chapter is a new scope.

03

Cancellation and refund

Cancel before source transfer or processing begins for a full refund. After work starts, only completed and delivered items are retained: USD 75 for source mapping and the style sheet, USD 100 for the print specimen, and USD 75 for the EPUB, validation, and correction pass. The undelivered balance is refunded.

04

Source handling and support

Working source copies are deleted within fourteen calendar days after delivery, cancellation, or refund unless the written scope requires earlier deletion. Customer material is never reused as public proof without separate permission. Support is limited to written clarification during the seven-day correction window.
