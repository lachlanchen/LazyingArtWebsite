A way to read

Use the translations as a handrail.

Read a short original passage first. Mark the particle, name, or reference that breaks the sense. Compare the other languages only when you are stuck, then return to 文言文 and restate the passage in your own words.

  1. ReadStay with one short original passage.
  2. MarkFind the exact place where the thread breaks.
  3. CompareUse the parallel languages to test your reading.
  4. ReturnGo back to the original and explain it plainly.

Why four languages

Each one reveals a different choice.

Classical Chinese preserves the compression. English often makes the sentence structure explicit. Modern Japanese and modern Chinese illuminate different continuities in wording, names, and interpretation. None replaces the original; together they make it easier to return to it.
