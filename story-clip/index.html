The fixed boundary

Editing one owned recording, not producing a campaign.

The USD 250 pilot excludes translation unless separately scoped, new filming, paid stock, publishing, raw source redistribution, extra finished clips, and performance or viral promises. It does not claim travel-client experience or turn project pieces into customer results.

You provide

The source recording, proof that you control the requested use, source language, names and protected terms, intended audience and platform, and any privacy or brand constraints.

We review

Duration, speech and picture usability, likely candidate moments, caption needs, third-party music, faces, voices, locations, marks, and whether the requested edit is rights-safe.

You decide

You choose one of the two timestamped candidates. A new source, different selected moment, additional language, second aspect ratio, or additional clip is a new scope.

Working terms

Review the source before setting the clock.

The accepted written scope controls the work. It records the two-stage delivery dates, source handling, cancellation and refund terms before payment or source transfer.

01

Timing

The candidate return date is agreed after a source and rights review. The final-clip date is agreed after the buyer selects one candidate and supplies any missing names or terms.

02

Correction pass

Send one consolidated list of caption, timing, or cut corrections within seven calendar days after delivery. A new source, candidate, edit direction, language, format, or version is outside the pass.

03

Source handling

Handling and deletion timing are accepted in writing before transfer. The raw recording is not redistributed, published, or reused as public proof by LazyingArt.
