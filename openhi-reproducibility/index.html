Correction pass

Cancel before private data transfer or processing begins for a full refund. Once work starts, the USD 500 price is allocated as USD 150 for the environment record, USD 225 for the reproduction packet, and USD 125 for the go/no-go report; only undelivered items are refunded.

Working data copies are deleted within fourteen calendar days after delivery, cancellation, or refund unless the accepted scope requires earlier deletion. Support is limited to written clarification during the correction window.
