EchoMind for Windows

Read posts and keep conversations with friends, groups and your Agent in one desktop window. Language cards display saved readings and grammar colors.

Unsigned preview. This download does not yet have a verified publisher signature or a Microsoft Store listing. Windows or your browser may warn about it. Keep your security protections enabled; use the web app if you prefer not to run an unsigned application.

Getting started

  1. Save the EXE in a folder you want to keep, then open it. No separate .NET installation or administrator access is needed.
  2. Sign in with your EchoMind username or email and password. You need an account with EchoMind access; this download does not grant an invitation.
  3. Choose Posts, Friends, Groups or AI Agent in the sidebar. Enable AI processing in Settings before using Agent; your account permissions and limits still apply.

Remembered sign-in is encrypted for your Windows user. Browser sessions stay separate. Use Settings to sign out.

What is included

Posts browsing with more items loaded as you scroll; recent friend, group and Agent conversations; text messages; saved language cards; light and dark appearance; and keyboard shortcuts. Ctrl+1–5 switches sections, Ctrl+R refreshes and Ctrl+Enter sends a message.

Still in development

This preview is smaller in scope than the phone apps. Native Google/Apple sign-in, voice and attachments, older-message paging, full post editing and reactions, explanation menus, automatic fetching of missing language cards, workspace-management controls and push notifications are not included. Account settings, mini programs and plans can open on the website. Subscriptions remain separate from this download.
