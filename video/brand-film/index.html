The deliverable

A film, a shorter cut, and the trail back to the sources.

The pilot is for footage that already exists but does not yet feel like one piece. It covers editorial assembly and finishing, not an open-ended generation campaign.

01

One 30–45 second master

A 16:9 H.264 master shaped around one agreed audience, message, and final action. Clips may be reordered, shortened, reframed, or left out.

02

One 25–30 second web cut

A shorter 16:9 cut derived from the same timeline, with its opening and ending adjusted so it still stands on its own.

The fixed boundary

Assembly of existing footage, not unlimited regeneration.

The USD 500 pilot does not include new filming, new AI generation, shot recreation, voice cloning, paid stock or music, translation, extra aspect ratios, channel publishing, or performance promises. Native Premiere Pro, After Effects, Final Cut, or DaVinci Resolve project delivery is included only when the accepted scope names the format and confirms compatibility.

Rights

02

Correction pass

03

Source handling

Transfer, confidentiality, retention, and deletion timing are accepted in writing. Customer footage is not reused as public proof without separate permission.
